How It Works

1. The Invite

Join our WhatsApp group to get notified about upcoming film days and stay in the loop.

2. Pic your Blick

A week before we meet, submit one of your favs or a film you really want to watch. 1 entry max per person and 10 movies total, so be fast to submit! We'll handle the logistics of renting or finding the stream.

3. Sell Your Picblick

On event day, everyone who submitted a film gets exactly one minute to pitch the club on why their movie deserves the screen.

4. The Final Showdown

We vote the list down to the top 2 films. The final two members go head-to-head with one last 1-minute pitch to secure the win.

5. Watch & Enjoy

The winning film is crowned, the lights go down, and the movie starts.

6. Rate & Review

After the credits roll, we discuss the film as a group and assign it a final, official PICBLICK cumulative rating to enter into the archive.

About Us

PICBLICK is never just about watching films. It's about finding people to talk about them with.

Founded in 2026 by two friends living in Munich, PICBLICK is a film club born out of a desire to share and learn about cinema in a fun and social environment. We provide an inclusive space for everyone, from casual moviegoers to dedicated cinephiles from around the world to connect and share perspectives after the credits roll.

Our focus is on exploring all facets of cinema, ranging from mainstream blockbusters to niche international and arthouse films. What sets PICBLICK apart is our democratic approach: every member has the opportunity to pitch a film and 'sell' the club on why we should watch it. This establishes a personal connection to the movies we select, keeping everyone engaged and ensuring our archive is driven by our members' passions. Thanks to our diverse and international group, Picblick is the perfect place to discover new genres, unique styles, and cinematic voices you might never explore on your own.
